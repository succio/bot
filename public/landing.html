FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What document types are supported?
We support Canadian bank statements (TD, Scotiabank, CIBC, RBC), payroll statements with full earnings and deduction breakdowns, employment verification letters, CRA Notice of Assessment documents, T4 Slips (Statement of Remuneration Paid) with auto-calculated CPP/QPP, EI, and PPIP deductions, and Void Cheques (BMO, Scotiabank, RBC, TD, CIBC).
How does pricing work?
You purchase a statement package (1, 3, or 6 months) and we generate that many consecutive monthly statements for you, each with realistic carry-over balances. Additional documents such as paystubs, T4 slips, or void cheques are $70 each. You can preview and edit everything before exporting to PDF.
What cryptocurrencies do you accept?
We accept Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, USDT (TRC20 & ERC20), Tron, BNB, Solana, XRP, Dogecoin, and many more through our payment processor. You can select your preferred cryptocurrency at checkout.
How long does payment take to process?
Payment confirmation depends on the blockchain network you use. Most payments are confirmed within 10-30 minutes. Once confirmed, tokens are automatically added to your account.
Can I customize every field?
Yes. Every field in every document is fully customizable including names, addresses, dates, amounts, transaction details, account numbers, and more. You see a live preview as you edit.
How do I contact support?
Join our Signal group for direct support and updates. Our team responds to all inquiries within 24 hours.
